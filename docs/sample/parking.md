# Parking and getting here

Sample data for the starter kit. Prairie Works Hall is a made-up venue, so do not drive to it.

Prairie Works Hall is at 1200 Example Avenue, Kansas City. The main entrance faces Example Avenue. The loading dock is on the north side.

## Where to park

Park in Lot C, the gravel lot on the north side of the building, next to the loading dock. Parking in Lot C is free all weekend for anyone wearing a hackathon badge. Put the paper badge on your dashboard so the attendant can see it.

Lot A, in front of the main entrance, is reserved for sponsors and judges. Cars without a sponsor pass are towed after 10 PM.

Street parking on Example Avenue is free after 6 PM on Friday and all day Saturday and Sunday. The meters on weekdays take cards and cost $2 an hour.

## Overnight

Lot C is open overnight. Lot A closes at midnight. If you sleep at the venue, move your car to Lot C before midnight on Friday.

## Other ways in

The bus stop at Example Avenue and 12th Street is one block south. Routes 25 and 47 stop there every 20 minutes until 11 PM.

Bike racks are inside the north door, next to the loading dock. Bring a lock.

Rideshare drop-off is at the main entrance. Ask your driver for "Prairie Works Hall, main entrance".
