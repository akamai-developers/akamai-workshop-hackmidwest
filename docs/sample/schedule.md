# Weekend schedule

Sample data for the starter kit. Prairie Works Hall is a made-up venue and these times are examples.

All events are in Prairie Works Hall unless noted. Times are Central Time.

## Friday

- 6:00 PM: Check-in opens at the registration desk by the main entrance. Pick up your badge and Wi-Fi card.
- 7:00 PM: Team formation mixer in the atrium, for anyone still looking for a team.
- 9:00 PM: Doors close for the night. Lot C stays open.

## Saturday

- 8:00 AM: Breakfast in the atrium.
- 9:00 AM: Opening ceremony in the main hall. Hacking starts when it ends.
- 12:30 PM: Lunch in the atrium.
- 2:00 PM: Akamai Cloud office hours at the sponsor tables, until 5:00 PM.
- 6:30 PM: Dinner in the atrium.
- 11:59 PM: Midnight snack and a ten-minute stretch break.

## Sunday

- 8:00 AM: Breakfast in the atrium.
- 12:00 PM: Submissions close on Devpost. Late submissions are not judged.
- 1:00 PM to 3:30 PM: Judging. Teams demo at their tables in the order on the screen.
- 4:00 PM: Closing ceremony and prizes in the main hall.
