# Rules

Sample data for the starter kit. These rules are examples, not the real event rules.

## Teams

Teams have one to five members. Every member needs a badge. You may change teams until the opening ceremony on Saturday at 9:00 AM.

## What you can build

Write your project's code during the event, after the opening ceremony. You may use open source libraries, public APIs, frameworks, and code you wrote before the event, if you say so in your submission. Templates and starter kits, like this one, are allowed.

## Submitting

Submit on Devpost before 12:00 PM on Sunday. A submission needs a project name, a short description, the list of team members, and a link to your code. A short demo video is optional and helps the judges.

## Demos

Each team gets three minutes to demo and two minutes for questions. Show the working project, not slides. If something breaks during the demo, say what it should do and move on.

## The Akamai Cloud challenge

Projects entered in the Akamai Cloud challenge are judged on one question: does the project integrate Akamai Cloud services? Show the judges each service in Cloud Manager or with a linode-cli command, and show it working in your demo.

## Conduct

Be kind. The code of conduct is printed on the back of your badge. Report a problem to any organizer in a yellow shirt.
